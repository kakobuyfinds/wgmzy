What to check before ordering

Save the exact item link, color, size and currency in your shortlist. Compare seller measurements with an item that already fits. When warehouse quality-control (QC) photos are available, check that they match the option you ordered.

Shoes

Confirm the size system and compare any stated insole length with a pair that fits. In QC photos, review the size label and look at both soles and toe boxes.

Hoodies and sweaters

Compare laid-flat chest width and body length. Double the flat chest width only when you need to compare it with a circumference.

T-shirts

Compare shoulder width and body length for the fit you want. Look at print or embroidery placement and check the color label.

Jackets

Measure over the layers you expect to wear. Review sleeve length, the main closure and any listed lining before committing.

Compare the delivered cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery to the warehouse, applicable agent fees, international postage and any estimated taxes or duties. Convert each line to the same currency before comparing quotes, and check which charges the quoted shipping route already includes.

For bulky items such as jackets or boxed shoes, ask for the packed dimensions and weight. Check the shipping route’s billing rules because the final charge can depend on dimensional weight and rounding.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is this KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is an independent browsing guide with selected CAFANS listings, category filters and buying checks. The product cards open on CAFANS.

How do I use a listing with KakoBuy?

Open the CAFANS product page and check whether KakoBuy is listed as an agent for that item. Confirm the source listing and chosen variant before placing an order.

Are the catalog prices the final checkout amounts?

The displayed amounts are CAFANS item estimates in USD. Check the current listing and request a shipping quote for your destination to estimate the delivered cost.

Where can I see QC photos?

Check the listing or your agent account for available warehouse photos. Compare the item label, color, measurements and visible condition against your order before approving shipment.
